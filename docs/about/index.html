Technical Stack & Philosophy

In an era dominated by multi-megabyte solutions, I advocate for radical minimalism and technical clarity:

  • Languages: I love Rust. I know most programmers hate its complexity, and I have to say, sometimes I understand why. But Rust is one of the few (popular) languages that try to be clean. That is, whenever Rust reports a compiler error, the same program in C or C++ causes a segfault, while in Python it either crashes or takes 10 times longer to run.
  • Problem solving: Always ask why before how to solve a problem. Often the question is wrong and must be rephrased before finding an answer.
  • Tech Stack: to be implemented.
